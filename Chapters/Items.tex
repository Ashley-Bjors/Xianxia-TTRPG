\subsection{Weapons}

Weapons are generally sorted into 4 different categories depending on their measure.

\textbf{Short weapons}: Daggers, Fans, Butterfly swords, (fists)\\
\textbf{Medium weapons}: Swords (Jians \& Daos), Axes, Hammers, (legs) \\
\textbf{Long weapons}: Staves, Guan dao, Spears \\
\textbf{Ranged weapons}: bows, crossbows, thrown weapons, hidden weapons \\
\textbf{Unorthodox weapons}: Shields, rope darts, chain whips, meteor hammers, three-section staves

the most common and socially expected are:\\
Jian (Double edged sword)\\
Dao (Single edged sword)\\
Qiang (Spear)\\
Gun (Staff)

With exceptions depending on style, skill and outside factors. Long weapons have an advantage over Medium weapons due to its range, which in turn out ranges Short weapons which outperforms Long weapons by moving within their minimum range.
