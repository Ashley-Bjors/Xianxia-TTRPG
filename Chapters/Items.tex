\subsection{Weapons}

Weapons are generally sorted into 4 different categories depending on their measure.

\textbf{Short weapons}: Daggers, Fans, Butterfly swords, (fists)\\
\textbf{Medium weapons}: Swords (Jians \& Daos), Axes, Hammers, (legs) \\
\textbf{Long weapons}: Staves, Guan dao, Spears \\
\textbf{Ranged weapons}: bows, crossbows, thrown weapons, hidden weapons \\
\textbf{Unorthodox weapons}: Shields, rope darts, chain whips, meteor hammers, three-section staves

the most common and socially expected are:\\
Jian (Double edged sword)\\
Dao (Single edged sword)\\
Qiang (Spear)\\
Gun (Staff)

With exceptions depending on style, skill and outside factors. Long weapons have an advantage over Medium weapons due to its range, which in turn out ranges Short weapons which outperforms Long weapons by moving within their minimum range.

\subsection{Treasures}

Any item with supernatural abilities or properties are considered treasures.

\subsubsection{Storage Rings}

A jade ring infused with spatial arts to store items inside it and retrieve it based on Qi signatures.

The internal storage within a ring depends on its rank. Items inside the ring spoil or degrade as if it was carried out in the open. 
If an item is too large for the remaining space the ring will simply refuse. 
An unknown ring can be emptied out an item at a time or all at once but the nature of an item inside can not be ascertained without memorization.

\subsubsection{Jade Slip}

A slate of jade malleable with Qi to write on. Once the message is finished it can be sent flying to a known recipient or location. 
When it arrives or runs out of Qi it is rendered inert with only a trace of the writers Qi to identify the validity.
