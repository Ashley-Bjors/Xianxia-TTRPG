\section{The Dao}

All Cultivators strive for understanding of the universe, this understanding is known as the Dao, or heavenly dao. Fully comprehending the dao is mostly beyond mortal comprehension, so cultivators decide to understand smaller fractions of the heavenly dao which is more manageable. There are elemental dao which requires understanding of every aspect of a specific element. Martial daos requires understanding all that their weapon entails. More esoteric dao, like the justice dao understands the full complexity of lawfulness

A higher understanding of one's dao eases the intake of Qi if the space allows it. It is also required to move beyond Energy storing and forming a core.

As one's understanding of a dao increases they start embodying it more. The more abstract the dao the more difficult it is to comprehend and requires extraordinary insights to comprehend. 

Some example of different Dao and their embodiments:

\subsection{Elemental Daos}

\textbf{Dao of Fire} – Skin turns ashen or glows faintly; hair flickers like flames; body is warm to the touch; eyes burn with embers; footsteps leave behind scorch marks—“I’m not sweating; I'm just radiating my fiery aura.” \\
\textbf{Dao of Water} – Skin is smooth and cool like jade; hair flows unnaturally, even without wind; voice has a serene, rippling quality; movements are fluid and hypnotic—“I don’t walk; I flow. Also, I spilled tea on your scrolls.”\\
\textbf{Dao of Earth} – Skin hardens like stone or takes on a metallic sheen; footsteps make the ground tremble slightly; veins glow with molten energy or crystalline light—“I’m not clumsy; I’m just grounded.”\\
\textbf{Dao of Wind} – Body feels weightless; hair is always tousled by an unseen breeze; voice carries like a whisper in the wind; afterimages appear when moving quickly—“I’m not late; I’m just wind-powered.”\\
\textbf{Dao of Lightning} – Hair crackles with static; pupils flicker like lightning; touch delivers tiny electric shocks; movements are unnaturally fast and precise—“I fried your talisman. My bad.”\\
\textbf{Dao of Ice} – Skin is cold as frost and may shimmer with frozen patterns; breath mists even in warmth; eyes are crystalline like frozen lakes; surroundings chill upon touch—“Want an ice cube?”


\subsection{Mystical Daos}

\textbf{Dao of the Moon} – Eyes glow softly like moonlight; body emits a faint silvery radiance at night; emotions seem distant; all nearby water bodies behave strangely—“I’m not moody; I’m just lunar. Also, the pond is glowing. Don’t ask.” \\
\textbf{Dao of the Sun} – Skin takes on a golden hue; sweat evaporates instantly; presence is warm and inspiring; voice carries an undeniable authority—“I’m not bossy; I’m just radiant.” \\
\textbf{Dao of Stars} – Eyes resemble a starry sky; tiny constellations appear on their skin—“I’m not glowing; I’m just stellar. Also, I think I just predicted your future. Spoiler: it’s bright.” \\
\textbf{Dao of Shadows} – Presence flickers in and out of vision; voice sometimes comes from different directions; reflections occasionally lag behind movements—“I’m not sneaky; I’m just shadowy. Also, I think I just scared your cat.” \\
\textbf{Dao of Dreams} – Eyes seem distant as if lost in a dream; body flickers between reality and illusion; presence makes others feel drowsy or disoriented—“I’m not zoning out; I’m just dreamy.” \\
\textbf{Dao of Death} – Skin becomes pale or corpse-like; breath is eerily silent; presence makes animals uneasy; flowers wilt slightly when they walk past—“I’m not creepy!” \\
\textbf{Dao of Life} – Skin is vibrant and filled with vitality; wounds heal unnaturally fast; plants flourish where they step; voice carries warmth and comfort—“I think I just grew a tree in your courtyard.” \\
\textbf{Dao of Time} – Presence feels ancient; shifts between youth and age; surroundings subtly distort, causing minor déjà vu in others—“I think I just made you relive breakfast. How was it?” \\
\textbf{Dao of Space} – Slight distortions appear around them; they seem to move without crossing the distance; objects sometimes shift position when unobserved—“I accidentally moved your chair. Where’d it go?”

\subsection{Martial Dao}

\textbf{Dao of the Sword} – Eyes gleam like sharpened steel; body feels as if it’s a honed blade; presence cuts through distractions; air subtly whistles when they move—“I’m not sharp; I’m just edgy.” \\
\textbf{Dao of the Fist} – Muscles ripple with hidden power; aura feels like an impending impact; hands leave behind aftershocks when striking—“I think I just cracked the floor. Again.” \\
\textbf{Dao of Blood} – Wounds heal rapidly but leave faint scar patterns; blood moves unnaturally when spilled; presence unnerves those with weak wills—“Oh, I’m bleeding…”\\
\textbf{Dao of War} – Eyes burn with battle intent; footsteps echo like marching soldiers; aura incites aggression or courage in those around them—“I’m not angry!”



\subsection{Abstract Daos}

\textbf{Dao of Karma} – Fate itself bends around them; good or bad luck follows in their wake; people feel inexplicably indebted or opposed to them—“I think I just cursed your dice. Good luck!” \\
\textbf{Dao of Illusions} – Presence is hard to focus on; voice sometimes echoes strangely; people find themselves questioning reality around them—“I’m not here. Go away!” \\
\textbf{Dao of Chaos} – Aura is unpredictable, shifting from calm to intense; objects sometimes behave strangely; luck fluctuates wildly in their presence—“Hey your tea is now soup.” \\
\textbf{Dao of Emotions} – Eyes reflect the emotions of those around them; presence subtly influences moods; voice can stir deep feelings even in the coldest hearts—“I’m not an emo!” \\
\textbf{Dao of the Beast} – Eyes resemble a predator’s; teeth sharpen slightly; presence feels wild and untamed; instincts become animalistic—“I don’t growl in my sleep.” \\
\textbf{Dao of Music} – Voice carries a mesmerizing rhythm; footsteps sound like distant melodies; their words linger in people’s minds like a song—“I think I just got your theme song stuck in your head. You’re welcome.”
