\section{Martial Arts}
A martial art is anything a cultivator does, which is reproducible and teachable. This means a Martial art can include just about anything. They are usually studied from a Manual, sometimes orally from an elder or created through trial and error. The Tier of an art does not necessarily correlate to its power but is an estimation on how ubiquitous it is. Tier 9 Being Available in any reputable cultivation shop or Sect to Tier 1 being only for the most valuable of core disciples of a major sect.
